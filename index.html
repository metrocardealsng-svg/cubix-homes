WHY WE
STARTED CUBIX.

CUBIX Homes and Investment Limited was founded in July 2024 by two young professionals who saw a gap in the Nigerian construction industry — a gap between what clients deserved and what was being delivered. Armed with architectural licenses, real estate experience, and an unwavering commitment to quality, Arc. Chimzi and Arc. Mohammed set out to build a company that would treat every project as a personal statement of excellence. Today, CUBIX has delivered high-profile projects across Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt — and this is only the beginning.
