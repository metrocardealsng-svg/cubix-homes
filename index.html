BUILT WITH
PURPOSE.

CUBIX Homes and Investment Limited is a construction and real estate company founded by two licensed architects with a knack for high-level, precise design, construction, and real estate.

Over a short period, CUBIX has handled high-profile construction projects across Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt — proving that quality and speed are not mutually exclusive.

"With a wealth of Real Estate experience and Building Professionalism by the licensed architects in charge, your building investment is at its safest under our belt."

Founded in July 2024, we set out to build a company that treats every project as a personal statement of excellence. Today, CUBIX has delivered high-profile projects across Nigeria — and this is only the beginning.
